# QnA packaging

**One-liner:** Invite a cohort. Run a fair quiz. See who needs help. Prove the outcome.

**Category:** Assessment & outcomes workbench for cohort-based training — faster than an LMS, smarter than Forms.

## Core — “Run the assessment”

- Create & publish quizzes  
- Invite cohort & track access  
- Fair timed attempt  
- Scores & basic results for learners  
- Basic cohort status  

**Pitch:** Relief from Google Forms chaos.

## Growth — “Operate and prove”

- Results workbench  
- Actionable morning follow-up  
- Integrity review (“Needs review”, never accusation)  
- Outcomes export / stakeholder snapshot  
- Hard-question insights  

**Pitch:** Relief from spreadsheets + awkward LMS reporting.

## Later — Knowledge edge

- Program docs → better question drafting / post-result reading  
- Practice mode separate from graded exams  

**Pitch:** Acceleration — not the identity of the product.

## Competitive contrast

| Need | Google Forms | LMS quiz | QnA |
|------|--------------|----------|-----|
| Launch fast | Strong | Weak | Strong |
| Fair timed cohort attempt | Weak | Medium | Strong |
| See who to chase tomorrow | Weak | Weak | **Strong** |
| Learner leaves smarter | Weak | Medium | **Strong** |
| Stakeholder-ready proof | Weak | Medium | **Strong** |
| Light enough for academies | Strong | Weak | Strong |

**Winning claim:** Forms start the job. LMS overcomplicates the job. QnA finishes the job.

## Value loops

1. **Launch** — every learner has a clear way in  
2. **Sit** — fair attempt finished  
3. **Learn** — learner understands the result  
4. **Coach** — operator takes a next step today  
5. **Prove** — one clear outcomes snapshot
